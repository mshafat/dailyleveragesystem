Daily Leverage System

Master Your Day. Achieve Your Vision.

The Daily Leverage System is a high-performance application designed to help you master your focus through The Daily Leverage Method. Created by Bangladeshi productivity enthusiast Munadil Shafat, this framework is one of the world’s simplest and most effective systems for goal achievement.

The Science of the Equation: 0 + 1 + 2 = 4

[Vision: 0] + [Lead: 1] + [Flow: 2] = [Victory: 4]

  • 0 (GOAL): Your North Star. Every action must serve your ultimate purpose.
  • 1 (MUST): Your Lead Action. The single most important move that defines your progress today.
  • 2 (NEXT): Two Flow Actions. Supporting moves that maintain your momentum.
  • = 4 (BEST): The Victory. One more act that transforms a productive day into your Best day.

This "One More" is your Leverage. It fuels a chain reaction that transforms vision into reality. Small daily wins lead to massive lifelong breakthroughs.

DISCLAIMER
This is a completely free and open-source application designed to gain leverage through daily focus. This app is not a replacement for traditional To-Do lists or Note-taking apps; rather, it is built to work alongside them. All your data is saved strictly in your local browser and is never stored on any server. Users are strongly advised to regularly export and back up their data at their own responsibility.
